## What changed

The old navigation had fourteen items. The new one has five. Everything else lives inside those five, organized by what a visitor is trying to do: find care, book a visit, or learn about a service.

## Built for the people running it

Staff can add events, update hours, and post news without touching code. Every field has a hint and a preview.
