## The hard part

Scheduling. Therapists, clients, rooms, and insurance rules all constrain each other. The calendar warns about conflicts before they happen and suggests the nearest open slot.

## Design notes

- Dense but readable: a 4px grid and one accent color
- Every table can be filtered, sorted, and exported
- Dark mode for the late-night note writers
