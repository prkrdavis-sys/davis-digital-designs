## Approach

Every section answers one question. The hero says what it is, the next says who it is for, and the last says how to start. Motion is used to guide the eye, never to show off.

## Highlights

- Scroll-linked hero illustration that blooms as you read
- Hand-tuned typography scale for mobile first
- Booking flow embedded without leaving the page
