## More than a game

The playful challenges are the hook. The product around them is the course: concepts in plain language, exercises that build on each other, and feedback that shows what to try next.

## What it includes

Guided lessons, practice prompts, progress that rewards showing up, and a challenge mode that uses the lesson you just finished.
