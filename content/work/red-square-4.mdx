## Design pillars

1. Instant restart. Death costs zero seconds.
2. Every level fits on one screen.
3. Juice everywhere: squash, stretch, particles, and a satisfying thunk.

## Tools

Vanilla TypeScript on a 2D canvas, a homemade level editor, and a tiny synth for sound effects so the whole game ships under 200 KB.
